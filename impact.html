Community impact

Building things should help people.

Engineering taught me how to make ideas real. Service taught me why that matters. Through student-government leadership, I helped support school-wide Make-A-Wish efforts that cleared five figures twice, including documented totals above $12,000 and $13,433.

My Eagle Scout project and other volunteer work also included park service, quilt tying, and food support connected to Ronald McDonald House programs near Primary Children's Hospital.

I am keeping that thread going by pointing visitors to causes I trust and have worked around before. If you want to help, donate directly through the official organization pages below.

How I help

Service, but with follow-through.

I am not an official charity representative, and this page is not pretending to be one. It is a clean place to send people when they ask what I care about and where they can help.

For donation receipts, tax details, recurring gifts, employer matches, or campaign-specific questions, use the official pages linked above.

  • Rally people clearly. Good fundraising works when the ask is simple, the goal is visible, and people understand where the money goes.
  • Connect service to execution. Planning, communication, supply runs, events, and follow-up matter as much as the headline number.
  • Keep it direct. Donations should go to official organization pages so supporters know exactly who is receiving the gift.
